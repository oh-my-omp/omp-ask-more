All questions of a call go into one form, one section each: its header, brief, evidence, diagram, risks and demos, then every option with its tradeoff, your `recommended` one marked, then the question and its choices. No choice is selected for the user; they pick an option or type their own answer. The answers return as this tool's result, and you continue in the same turn.

Write for someone who has not read this session. Put the independent questions of a round in one call, or one question per call when the instruction asks one at a time; a question whose options depend on an answer goes in a later call. A rejected call lists every problem: fix them and call `ask` again.
