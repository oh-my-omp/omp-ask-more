All questions of a call go into one form, one section each: its header, brief, evidence, diagram, risks and demos, then the question and its choices, each choice with its tradeoff, description, holds-when and cost on the lines under it and your `recommended` one marked. No choice is selected for the user; they pick an option or type their own answer. The answers return as this tool's result, and you continue in the same turn.

A demo is either frames you write in Markdown, shown in order, or any file you name: a picture is shown in the form, any other file, such as a video or a recording, as its `file://` URL the user opens.

Write for someone who has not read this session. Put the independent questions of a round in one call, or one question per call when the instruction asks one at a time; a question whose options depend on an answer goes in a later call. A rejected call lists every problem: fix them and call `ask` again.
